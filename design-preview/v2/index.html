Our own product

FUFU Pet Care

Because they are family

We don't only build for clients. FUFU Pet Care is a pet-care app we designed, built and run ourselves, from the AI model to the app and the clinic portal.

  • AI skin and coat screening from a photo, with 4 urgency levels
  • FUFU AI Vet: ask about your pet any time, 24 hours
  • A health record for each pet: vaccines, deworming, weight and reminders
  • Cases referred to partner clinics with photos and history, through the FUFU Partner Portal
94% accuracy of the skin screening model, tested on 6,215 vet-confirmed images

Preliminary screening only, not a diagnosis. A veterinarian makes the final call.

Open the web app fufupetcare.com
